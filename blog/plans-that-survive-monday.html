Plans that survive Monday

A quarterly plan is usually a document that was true on the afternoon it was presented. By Monday, a hire slipped, a dependency moved, and nobody wants to open the deck.

The gap is the product

Teams do not fail because they cannot write goals. They fail because the goal, the work, and the forecast live in three tools. Coplanner is Pichho’s attempt to hold that middle object — the plan — so a change in one place is visible in the others.

What “living” means

It does not mean the plan rewrites itself overnight. It means owners, dates, and risks are editable in the same place leadership already looks. AI can propose the next draft. A person still accepts it.
