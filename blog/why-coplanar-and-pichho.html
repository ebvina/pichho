Why Coplanar AI and Pichho are different names

People ask if we are Coplanar AI or Pichho. The answer is both, on purpose.

Coplanar AI Pty Ltd is the business: contracts, careers, support, this website. Pichho is the product teams log into. If we ever ship a second product, customers should not have to relearn who we are.

It is also clearer in a sales conversation. “Coplanar AI builds Pichho” is a complete sentence. “We are an AI company” is not.
