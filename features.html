Features

Everything a plan needs to stay honest.

Coplanner is intentionally narrow. It does planning well, then gets out of the way of the tools you already ship in.

AreaWhat you get
Living plansGoals, work, owners, dates, and dependencies on one object.
AI draftsPlan from a brief, rewrite a slipped quarter, summarise a review.
ScenariosCompare base / stretch / cut without forking the whole file.
ReviewsWeekly notes attached to the same goals. No new deck each Monday.
PermissionsWorkspace, team, and guest views. Clients see what you share.
AuditWho changed the date, the owner, or the forecast — and when.
ExportClean PDF or CSV when someone still needs a pack.
IntegrationsCalendar, issue trackers, and docs. More as teams ask.

Editor, not oracle

AI proposes. People accept. Every generated change is visible before it lands.

Fast on purpose

A static-feeling UI on a serious planning model. No chrome for chrome’s sake.

Built for review
