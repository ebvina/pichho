Security

Security is part of the product, not a PDF we send later.

Coplanner holds plans, forecasts, and sometimes headcount. We treat that as confidential business data.

Access

Company plans include SSO and admin-managed roles. Guests only see workspaces you share. Audit logs are available on Company.

Data

Customer data is used to provide Coplanner, not to train public models. AI drafts run against your workspace context. You can export or request deletion.

Infrastructure

Production systems use encrypted transport and encrypted storage. Access to production is limited to a small Pichho engineering group and is logged.

Reports

If you believe you have found a vulnerability, email hello@pichho.com with “Security” in the subject. Please do not publish details until we have had a chance to respond.
